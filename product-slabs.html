What are Himalayan cooking slabs used for?
Himalayan cooking slabs are thick blocks of pink salt used for cooking, searing, and serving food. When heated, they retain and distribute heat evenly, giving meats, vegetables, and seafood a unique, subtly salty flavor. They can also be chilled and used as serving platters for sushi, cheese, or desserts.
How do I heat and care for my cooking slab?
Heat the slab gradually on a stovetop or in the oven over low to medium heat — never place a cold slab directly on high heat or it may crack. After use, let it cool completely, then wipe clean with a damp cloth and dry thoroughly. Do not wash with soap or submerge in water.
Will the slab make my food too salty?
The salt imparts a very subtle seasoning, not an overwhelming taste. For best results, avoid adding extra salt to your dish. If you're sensitive to salt, you can oil the slab lightly before cooking or use it primarily for presentation.
